On air

YouTube radio

Endless mixes made from what you like on YouTube. Songfinite finds the sounds in your listening (nightcore, rock versions, the artists you love) and turns them into mixes that never run out. Keep scrolling and new shelves of mixes keep tuning in.

  • Daily mixes that change every day, Because you like an artist, Discover mixes of artists you have never played, and mixes for your late nights, mornings, workouts and focus.
  • Each mix shows the artists inside before you play it.
  • Like and dislike any song, block an artist, and slide each mix between familiar songs and new ones. Songs you skip early count too.
  • ADD keeps a song's link in your songs, DOWNLOAD keeps it offline, and every song has the full menu: playlists, favourites, lyrics and more.
  • Your taste is read through Google sign-in, or straight through the YouTube sign-in of a browser installed on your computer (Firefox, Chrome, Edge, Opera GX, Brave, Vivaldi and others).

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. When you do, Songfinite asks Google for read-only access to your YouTube account, so the radio can read the videos you liked, your playlists and the channels you follow. That information is used only to build your mixes, on your own computer. Without Google sign-in, the radio can read the same from the YouTube sign-in of a browser on your computer, or learn from what you play in Songfinite. Songfinite has no servers: nothing you sign in with or listen to is sent to its developer or to anyone else.

Songfinite uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles your data under the Google Privacy Policy.
