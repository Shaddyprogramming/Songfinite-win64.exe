Songfinite

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026 · Songfinite 1.31.4

Songfinite is a desktop music player that runs on your own computer. It has no servers and no accounts of its own. This page explains what it does with your data, in particular the data it can read from your Google account when you choose to sign in.

In short: everything stays on your computer. Songfinite never sends your Google or YouTube data, your listening or your files to its developer or to anyone else, and never sells or shares it.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. Songfinite works without it. When you sign in, Google asks you to allow these permissions:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture (openid, email, profile), shown on Songfinite's account page so you can see which account is signed in.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly), read-only. Songfinite reads the videos you liked, your playlists and their songs, and the channels you subscribe to.

Songfinite cannot change anything on your Google or YouTube account: it cannot post, like, subscribe, upload, delete or edit playlists. Songfinite notes which of these permissions you allowed, so it can tell you when YouTube access was left out.

How the data is used

The YouTube data is used for one thing only: to find the kinds of music you listen to (for example nightcore, rock versions or a favourite artist) and to build the endless mixes in Songfinite's YouTube radio. This happens on your computer. The result, a list of your "sounds", the artists you listen to most and the songs mixes start from, is kept in Songfinite's data folder on your computer.

Your YouTube sign-in from a browser

So that YouTube treats you as yourself and not as a bot, Songfinite can borrow the YouTube sign-in of a browser on your computer, or use a cookies.txt you import yourself. You can turn this off on the account page.

  • Which browsers: to list the browsers you can choose from, Songfinite looks for the data folders of browsers installed on your computer (such as Firefox, Chrome, Edge, Opera GX, Brave or Vivaldi) and the names of their profiles. It does not open or read anything else in them.
  • What is copied: from the browser and profile you pick, only YouTube's and Google's cookies are copied, into Songfinite's data folder. Nothing else from the browser is read or kept. To find the browser of the Google account you signed in with, Songfinite asks Google which account those cookies belong to.
  • What it is used for: playing and downloading the songs you choose, and, for the radio, reading your liked videos, watch history, playlists and the channels you follow directly from YouTube, the same way your browser shows them to you. Your watch history is used, like everything else, only to build your mixes on your computer.

What is stored, and where

Everything below is kept on your own computer, in Songfinite's data folder (Documents/ShaddyGames/Songfinite) unless said otherwise:

  • The sign-in key Google hands back is kept in your system's password vault (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain or the Linux secret service) when one is available, otherwise in Songfinite's data folder. Your Google password never reaches Songfinite: you type it on Google's own page in your browser.
  • Your profile (name, email, picture) and your taste, with a short note of where it was read from and whether each part worked.
  • The borrowed YouTube sign-in: only YouTube's and Google's cookies, as described above.
  • An optional watch history, only if you import your own watch-history.json from Google Takeout.
  • What you tell the radio: songs you liked or disliked, artists and songs you blocked, songs skipped early, the mixes you tuned in to, and what the mixes on the radio's home held.
  • Your listening stats: for every song you hear, when, for how long, whether you skipped it and where it came from. You can export it as a CSV file yourself.
  • Your storage locations: the folders and drives you add, with how many songs they hold. Songfinite walks only those folders, to find your songs.

Who it is shared with

No one. Songfinite talks only to Google and YouTube, directly from your computer, to sign you in, read your YouTube data and play the songs you choose; to lyrics and music sites to look up lyrics and song details; and to Songfinite's own home page on GitHub Pages, about once a day, to see whether a newer version is out. That check only downloads the page: it sends nothing about you. You can turn it off in the settings. Songfinite has no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

YouTube API Services

Songfinite uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Songfinite keeps what it reads from the YouTube API (your taste and the songs mixes start from) only on your computer, and reads it again from YouTube once a day at most, unless you press READ AGAIN. Forgetting your taste on the account page, or disconnecting, deletes it at once.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Songfinite's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide the features described above, is not transferred to others, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

Removing your data

  • In Songfinite, open the account page and press Disconnect everything. This signs you out, asks Google to cancel the key, deletes the borrowed YouTube sign-in and forgets your taste.
  • In the settings, under Radio, you can forget your likes, dislikes, early skips and recent mixes, and allow blocked artists and songs again.
  • You can also remove Songfinite's access at any time from your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling Songfinite and deleting its data folder removes everything it kept.
